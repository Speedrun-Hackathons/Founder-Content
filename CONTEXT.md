# Founder Content

The language of a site where a founder turns a company URL into ads that are tested and scheduled to social accounts.

## Language

**Founder**:
The person who signs in and runs the site for one company: a solo founder or a member of a small team.
_Avoid_: user, customer, marketer

**Company**:
The business a Founder promotes, identified by its public URL.
_Avoid_: client, brand, project

**Document**:
A file the Founder uploads to back up the Brief, such as a deck or a product sheet.
_Avoid_: attachment, source

**Brief**:
The product description pulled from the Company URL and its Documents, approved by the Founder before anything is generated.
_Avoid_: summary, scrape, context

**Voice**:
The tone and wording rules derived from the Brief that every generated text follows.
_Avoid_: style, persona, brand voice

**Carousel**:
An Ad made of several images shown in order on one Platform.
_Avoid_: slideshow, gallery

**Run**:
One generation pass over an approved Brief that produces Variants for the chosen platforms.
_Avoid_: job, batch, campaign

**Ad**:
One piece of content for one platform: text with an image, or text with a video.
_Avoid_: post, creative, asset

**Variant**:
One of the two Ads a Run produces per platform, labeled A or B, so the two can be compared.
_Avoid_: version, option

**Post**:
A Variant handed to a platform with a send time.
_Avoid_: publish, schedule (as nouns)

**Platform**:
One social network a Post goes to: X, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Bluesky or Threads.
_Avoid_: channel, network

**Winner**:
The Variant the Founder picks by hand after comparing A and B.
_Avoid_: best, selected

**Run time**:
The minutes from pasting the Company URL to the first Post leaving, stamped by the site for every Run.
_Avoid_: duration, latency

**Time saved**:
The hours a Founder spends on content by hand each week, from published surveys, minus the Run time.
_Avoid_: efficiency, ROI
